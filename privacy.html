AutoPilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

AutoPilot for iPhone and Android (“the app”) is made by Elevatescale Technologies Private Limited (“we”, “us”). This page explains what the app uses, where it goes and how to delete it. Everything below applies to both versions; Android differences are listed at the end.

What the app uses

  • Audio you record. The app records from your microphone only after you tap Start and keeps recording until you tap Stop. While it records, iPhone shows it on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Things you type. Your name (so notes say your name instead of “Speaker 1”), your own thoughts on a note, words you add to “My words”, tasks, and the questions you ask.

There are no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, and no analytics or tracking. We don’t sell or share your data for advertising.

Where it goes

  • Soniox (speech to text). Audio is sent to Soniox, Inc. over an encrypted connection to be written down. The app deletes the uploaded audio and transcript from Soniox as soon as the text comes back.
  • Groq (AI writing). The transcript text, your name and “My words” are sent to Groq, Inc. over an encrypted connection to write titles, summaries and task lists, and to answer your questions.
  • Other AI providers, only if you choose. In Settings you can pick Apple’s on-device model, or add your own OpenAI or Google Gemini key. If you add your own key, transcript text goes to that provider instead.
  • Your iPhone. Notes, transcripts, summaries, tasks and chats are stored only on your iPhone. Audio files are deleted from your iPhone after they are written down.

We don’t run our own servers for your notes and we don’t receive a copy of them.

Use with Claude: the current iPhone version does not send your notes to any server of ours. If we add an optional “Use with Claude” setting later, it will be off by default, we will explain it in the app before you turn it on, and we will update this page first.

Keeping and deleting data

Your notes stay on your iPhone until you delete them. Delete one note by opening it › ⋯ › Delete note, or delete everything in Settings › Delete all notes. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Permissions

  • Microphone – to record after you tap Start.
  • Notifications – optional, for “new task” alerts.
  • Live Activities – optional, to show recording on the Lock Screen.

AutoPilot for Android

The Android app works the same way as above, with these differences:

  • Recording controls. While it records, Android shows an ongoing notification. You can stop recording from that notification, the Quick Settings tile or the app.
  • Call recordings made by your phone (optional). If you turn on “Capture calls”, the app reads the audio files your phone’s own call recorder saves (for example in a “Call recordings” folder), and a folder you pick if you choose one. It only picks up recordings made after you turn this on (plus the hour before). The app does not record calls itself, and it does not delete your phone’s call recordings.
  • Contact name or number in file names. If your phone puts the contact’s name or number in a call recording’s file name, that name or number is saved with the note on your phone and may appear in the transcript text sent to Groq.
  • No other AI providers. The Android app uses only Soniox and Groq as described above.
  • Android backup. Recordings and settings are excluded from Android backups. If you have Android backup to your Google account turned on, Android may include your notes database in that backup.
  • Permissions. Microphone (to record after you tap Start); Notifications (recording controls and “new task” alerts); Music and audio (only if you turn on “Capture calls”, to find your phone’s call recordings); Run at startup (to remind you to start recording again after a restart; the app never starts recording on its own).

As on iPhone, the Android app sends nothing to servers of ours, and deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone.

Other people’s consent

Please tell people when you record them and follow the law where you are. Some places need everyone’s consent before a conversation or call is recorded.

Children

AutoPilot is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

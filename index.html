AutoPilot

Notes from what you say

Tap Start and talk. AutoPilot writes down what was said and turns it into a short note with a title, a summary and your to-do list. Ask it questions like “What did I promise Ravi?” Works with English, Hindi and Telugu.

Quick tips

  • Tap Start to record and Stop to save the note.
  • Allow the microphone when the app asks. You can change this in iPhone Settings › AutoPilot.
  • To add to an old note, open it and tap Continue recording.
  • To delete everything, go to Settings in the app › Delete all notes.
